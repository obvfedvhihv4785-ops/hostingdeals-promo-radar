An independent price tracker for cloud hosting and VPS plans. It is not a hosting company: it does not sell plans, take orders or handle billing.

What this site does

cloudhostdeals reads the public pricing and promotion pages that cloud hosting and VPS providers publish for everyone, and republishes the prices, discounts and promo codes those pages state. Every listing links back to the exact page it was copied from, so any number here can be checked against its own source.

Where the numbers come from

An automated job fetches each provider's own public page — a pricing page, a promo page, or a public sitemap or JSON endpoint the provider publishes — and stores what that page says. Nobody types prices in by hand, and no figure on this site is an estimate.

When a source cannot be read, the site says so instead of filling the gap. Some providers serve their prices with JavaScript or put a bot check in front of their pricing page; those appear in the “Source status” table on the home and compare pages with the reason, and no price is published for them.

The reader respects each site's robots.txt. It does not impersonate a browser, does not solve or bypass bot checks, and does not use any provider's private interface.

How often it updates

Every six hours. A scheduled build re-reads every source, regenerates the pages and publishes them; the pipeline lives in a public repository linked in the footer of this site. The timestamp at the bottom of each page is the time of the last successful read, not the time you loaded the page.

What this site will not do

  • It will not publish a price it could not read. A missing number stays missing.
  • It will not invent a discount, a promo code or an expiry date.
  • It will not keep an expired promotion listed as if it were still running.
  • It will not describe a price as guaranteed. Providers change prices without notice.

How it is funded

Outbound provider links may be affiliate links. If you sign up through one, the provider may pay this site a commission; what you pay is the same either way. Listings are not paid placements — a provider cannot buy a spot on this site, and no provider can pay to have a competitor's listing removed.

Who is behind it

cloudhostdeals is a small independent project. It is published under the site name only: no personal names, phone numbers or postal addresses appear anywhere on this site. Questions, corrections and complaints go to the address on the contact page.
