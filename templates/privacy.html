Privacy policy

cloudhostdeals is a static website with no accounts, no forms and no newsletter. There is no way to send it personal information, and it does not sell, rent or trade visitor data.

Last updated ${policy_updated}

The short version

  • This site has no sign-up, no login, no comment box, no newsletter and no contact form.
  • It sets no cookies of its own and runs no analytics or statistics product.
  • It does not sell, rent or share visitor data. There is nothing to sell.
  • Third-party advertising is planned for the future. That will change what is described below, and this page will be updated to name the vendors before any ad code goes live.

What the site itself collects

Nothing. Every page is a pre-built HTML file. The only script element anywhere on the site is a JSON-LD block that describes the page for search engines; it does not execute tracking code and it does not read or store anything about you. The site sets no cookies of its own, and nothing on it stops working if you disable cookies or JavaScript.

What the hosting provider processes

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare processes each request as the hosting provider, including its standard security and performance features, under Cloudflare's own privacy policy. Because no analytics product is installed on top of it, this site has no per-visitor reporting, no visitor profiles, and no way to look up an individual reader.

Search engines

The site asks search engines to index its public pages through robots.txt and sitemap.xml, and it carries a Google Search Console verification tag so the owner can see aggregate search performance inside Google's own console. That tag does not run code on your device and does not collect personal information from visitors.

Advertising, including third-party ads

This site currently displays no advertising. It is planned to add third-party advertising later. When that happens, the following will apply, and this page will be updated first:

  • Third-party ad vendors will be able to set their own cookies, or read device and browser identifiers, in order to select, deliver and measure ads.
  • Those vendors operate under their own privacy policies rather than this one, and they will be named on this page along with links to their opt-out mechanisms.
  • Ads may be personalised using your prior visits to this and other websites, unless you turn personalised advertising off in the vendor's settings or in your browser.
  • You can block or delete these cookies at any time in your browser settings. Doing so does not stop you reading any part of this site.

Affiliate links

Outbound links to providers may be affiliate links. When you follow one, the provider may record that the visit came from this site and may pay a commission; the price you pay is not affected. This site never receives, stores or processes your account or payment details — those stay between you and the provider.

Your choices

You can read every page here with cookies disabled, with JavaScript disabled, or through a content blocker, because nothing on the site depends on them. Since the site holds no personal data about you, there is no profile to correct or delete. If you believe something here does concern your data, write to the address below.

Children

This site is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

The date at the top of this page is when it last changed. If advertising, analytics or any other data-collecting feature is added, this page will be revised and that date updated before the feature goes live.
