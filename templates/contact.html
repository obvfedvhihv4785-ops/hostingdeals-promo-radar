What to write about

What this site cannot help with

It is not the provider. Orders, payments, invoices, refunds, cancellations, account access and technical support all belong to the provider you bought from, so contact them directly. This site has no access to any provider account and cannot change anything about your service.

Before you write

If you are reporting a price that looks wrong, including the URL of the page you were looking at helps a lot. This site can only compare what it published against what the provider's page says right now, and it needs the source page to do that.
